Welcome to Swiftboox iOS Bengali eBook Reader !! 
 

Introduction
Swiftboox iOS app is the eBook reader app for eBooks purchased from our web store.

This app does not allow any purchase of eBooks or download samples. For all purchases please visit the website.

 

How to use
● When you install and open the app, you will get the default Library page.
● Before you login, please ensure that you have already registered in our website.
● Click on the Refresh icon on the right-hand-top corner and you will get the login screen.
● Login with your email and password that you have used for registering in the site.
● If you have forgotten your password, click on Forgot Password link and an external browser will open to take you to our website. You can then click on side-menu, click on your profile photo and then scroll below for logout button. After that, from the login screen, you can click on the Forgot Password link and follow on-screen instructions.
● After you are successfully logged in, the app will start syncing all your purchased and samples from your cloud library and download to your device.
● If you would like to browse and buy new books, click on the middle icon - Store and an external browser will open to take you to our website.
● After a book purchase, you can return to the Swiftboox app and click on the Refresh icon. It will download the new book to your device.
● That's It !! No more complex stuff between you and your favorite book for start reading !!
